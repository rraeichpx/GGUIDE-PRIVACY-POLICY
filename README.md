# Privacy Policy and Clarification Text for GGuide

**Last Updated:** 25 August 2026  
**Developer:** RR&LV Studios

RR&LV Studios (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the mobile application **GGuide** (“the App”).

This Privacy Policy and Clarification Text explains what information is collected through GGuide, why it is processed, how it is protected, which third-party services are used, how long certain information may be retained, and what rights users have regarding their personal data.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”).

Where consent is legally required, such as for certain advertising or privacy choices, the App may request consent separately through the appropriate consent interface.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect and process information necessary to provide GGuide’s features, secure the platform, prevent abuse, process purchases, display advertisements, and improve the App.

### 1.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create and use a GGuide account, we may process:

- Email address
- Firebase User ID (UID)
- Username
- Authentication and account status information

Authentication is handled through **Google Firebase Authentication**.

We do **not** store your Firebase Authentication password ourselves.

---

### 1.2 In-App Account and Gameplay Data

GGuide may store account-specific information in Firebase, including:

- Username and profile-related information
- Coins, Diamonds, tickets, and other virtual currency balances
- Owned cosmetic items and inventory
- Chest opening history
- Battle Pass and seasonal progression
- Giveaway participation records
- Daily reward and calendar progression
- Mini-game progress, results, scores, and related gameplay information
- Leaderboard rankings
- Referral-related account state
- Promo-related account state
- Notification-related account preferences
- Other gameplay or account progression required for GGuide features

This information is generally associated with your GGuide account and Firebase UID.

---

### 1.3 User-Generated Content

GGuide includes features that allow users to interact with other users.

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

- In-app chat messages
- Other user-generated text
- Gameplay-related user content

This information may be associated with your account for purposes including:

- Providing chat and community features
- Moderation
- Abuse prevention
- Enforcement of platform rules
- Security investigations

GGuide may use automated filters, moderation systems, reporting systems, mute restrictions, bans, or similar safeguards to protect users and the platform.

---

### 1.4 Device and Security Information

GGuide may process device-level information used for security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, and enforcement purposes.

This may include:

- Device identifiers used by GGuide
- Device-level security identifiers
- Device model
- Operating system information
- App integrity/security information
- Anti-abuse and anti-fraud records
- Device ban records
- Promo code usage records associated with a device
- Referral reward usage records associated with a device
- Security and audit logs

Device-level promo and referral usage information may be retained even after an account is deleted when necessary to prevent users from repeatedly deleting and recreating accounts in order to receive the same promotional or referral rewards multiple times.

Security and audit information may also be retained where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, enforce bans, prevent fraud, or protect GGuide and its users.

These retained records are not used for direct marketing.

## 2. Purchases and Transaction Information

GGuide offers in-app purchases through:

- **Google Play Billing** on Android
- **Apple StoreKit / App Store In-App Purchases** on iOS

We may process transaction-related information including:

- Product ID
- Purchase or transaction identifier
- Purchase status
- Purchase date
- Platform
- Transaction validation information
- Refund or revocation information
- Entitlement information

On Apple platforms, GGuide may verify signed App Store transaction information and communicate with Apple’s App Store Server services.

On Android, GGuide may verify purchase information using Google Play services.

### Payment Information

Your payment card number, bank account information, and other direct payment credentials are handled by Apple or Google.

**GGuide does not receive or store your full credit card number or bank account information.**

Certain transaction identifiers and purchase audit records may be retained after account deletion when necessary for:

- Preventing the same purchase from being rewarded more than once
- Preventing purchase replay or fraud
- Processing refunds or revocations
- Financial or accounting requirements
- Security investigations
- Compliance with applicable legal obligations

Where possible, retained transaction records are limited to the information required for these purposes.

## 3. Advertising and Advertising-Related Data

GGuide uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google Mobile Ads configuration, Google AdMob may process information such as:

- Approximate or coarse location derived from information such as an IP address
- Device identifiers
- Advertising-related data
- Information about advertisements shown to you
- Product interaction and app usage information
- Device and operating system information
- Performance information
- Diagnostic information
- Crash-related information

Advertising may include personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on applicable law, your region, your consent choices, and the capabilities available on your device.

### Advertising Consent

Where required, GGuide uses Google’s **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to request and manage advertising-related privacy choices.

Users in applicable regions may be presented with options to:

- Consent
- Decline consent
- Manage privacy choices

Where available and required, users may reopen their privacy choices through the App.

GGuide does not currently require Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission solely in order to display advertisements. Advertising may continue without access to Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).

## 9. Account Deletion

GGuide allows users to initiate deletion of their account from within the App.

When an account deletion is successfully completed:

- The Firebase Authentication account is deleted.
- User-specific account data associated with the deleted UID is removed where it is no longer required.
- Account progression is removed.
- Virtual currency balances are removed.
- Inventory and account-owned items are removed.
- Mini-game and account progression data associated with the account is removed where applicable.
- The user is signed out and can no longer use the deleted account.

### Information That May Be Retained After Account Deletion

Certain limited records may remain after account deletion when necessary for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, transaction-integrity, or legal purposes.

These may include:

#### Device-Level Promo and Referral Records

Device-level records indicating that a promotional code or referral reward has already been used may be retained.

This prevents a user from deleting an account, creating another account on the same device, and repeatedly claiming rewards intended to be available only once.

#### Security and Audit Logs

Security and audit records may be retained when necessary to:

- Detect or investigate fraud
- Investigate abuse
- Enforce device or account bans
- Protect other users
- Protect GGuide infrastructure
- Maintain platform integrity

#### Purchase and Transaction Records

Certain transaction identifiers or purchase audit information may be retained where necessary to:

- Prevent transaction replay
- Prevent the same transaction from generating rewards multiple times
- Process refunds and revocations
- Investigate fraud
- Meet accounting, tax, or other legal obligations

We do not retain deleted account information merely for advertising or direct marketing purposes.

## 10. Data Retention

Active account information is generally retained while necessary to provide GGuide services.

When an account is deleted, account-specific data that is no longer necessary is deleted or otherwise removed from active account functionality.

Certain limited information may be retained for longer periods where reasonably necessary for:

- Fraud prevention
- Abuse prevention
- Platform security
- Audit integrity
- Purchase replay prevention
- Refund or revocation handling
- Accounting requirements
- Tax requirements
- Legal obligations
- Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims

We do not state a fixed retention period where the appropriate period depends on the security, transaction, accounting, or legal purpose involved.

Information retained for these purposes is not retained simply to continue normal use of the deleted account.
