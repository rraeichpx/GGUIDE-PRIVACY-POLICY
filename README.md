# **Privacy Policy and Explicit Consent Text (Clarification Text) for GGuide**

**Last Updated:** 30 March 2026  
**Developer:** RR&LV Studios  

RR&LV Studios (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates the mobile application **GGuide** (“the App”).  
This Privacy Policy and Clarification Text explains how we collect, use, process, and protect user data within the App in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).  

By creating an account, checking the consent box during registration, and using the App, you explicitly agree to the collection and processing of your data as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

---

## **1. Information We Collect**
We collect only the minimal information required to operate the App’s features securely and fairly.

### **1.1 Firebase Authentication & Identity**
When creating an account, we collect:
- Email address  
- Firebase User ID (UID)

We do **not** store passwords. Authentication is handled securely by **Firebase Authentication**.

### **1.2 In-App Data (Firebase Realtime Database)**
The App stores the following user-specific data:
- Username  
- In-app currency balances (Coins & Diamonds)  
- Cosmetic items owned and chest opening history  
- Giveaway participation records  
- App progress and leaderboard rankings
- Legal consent logs (Timestamp of when you accepted this policy)

We do **not** store sensitive personal information such as real names, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

### **1.3 Device Information**
We collect your Device ID. This data is strictly used for security purposes to prevent fraud, cheating, and multi-account abuse (e.g., ensuring a single device cannot exploit promo codes or manipulate giveaways).

### **1.4 Financial & Transaction Data**
When you make an in-app purchase, we process purchase tokens securely provided by Google Play. We do **not** collect, process, or store your credit card or bank account information.

### **1.5 Non-Personal Data**
We may collect anonymous technical information, such as:
- Device model & Operating system  
- Crash reports  

This data **cannot identify the user** and is only used to improve app stability.

---

## **2. How We Use Collected Data**
We use collected data exclusively for the following purposes:
- Creating and managing your user account securely.
- Managing user progress, virtual currency, and unlocked items.
- Keeping your data synchronized across devices.
- Preventing fraud, cheating, and multi-account abuse (via Device ID banning).
- Operating core features like leaderboards, giveaways, and the in-app market.
- Processing in-app purchases securely.
- Serving personalized and non-personalized advertisements.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties for their direct marketing purposes.

---

## **3. Third-Party Services**
To provide our services, your data is securely stored and processed using trusted third-party infrastructure providers. The App uses:

- **Google Firebase:** For authentication, real-time database, and cloud functions.  
- **Google AdMob:** For displaying advertisements.  
- **Google Play Billing:** For verifying and processing in-app purchases.  

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy:  
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## **4. Data Security**
We take user data protection seriously. Firebase ensures:
- **Encryption in transit and at rest** - **Secure HTTPS communication** - **Strict database access rules (UID-based protection)** No unauthorized party can access another user’s information.

---

## **5. Children’s Privacy**
The App is not intended for children under **13 years old**.  
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under this age.  
If such data is discovered, it will be deleted immediately from our servers.

---

## **6. User Rights & Explicit Consent**
Under KVKK and GDPR, users have the right to:
- Request access to their stored personal data.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request the deletion of their account and associated data.

By registering for an account and checking the explicit consent box, you acknowledge that you have read this Clarification Text and consent to the processing of your data.

## **7. Data Retention**
User data is retained only for as long as your account remains active.  
When an account is deleted, all associated data (coins, diamonds, username, items, progress, device logs) is permanently erased from our active servers within 30 days, except for data required to be kept by legal or tax obligations (e.g., anonymous purchase history logs).
